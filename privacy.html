Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026.

Recall Radar does not currently require an account, collect email subscriptions, or operate its own vehicle database. When you submit a VIN, your browser sends it to the NHTSA VIN-decoding service. Vehicle make, model, and year are used to query NHTSA recall campaign data. Those third-party services may process request metadata under their own policies.

If you save a vehicle, its VIN, label, and recall campaign snapshots are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. They are not synced across devices or sent to a Recall Radar server. Anyone with access to this browser profile may access saved VINs. Remove a vehicle from My Garage or clear this site's browser storage to erase local data.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, as part of hosting. We do not currently run advertising or analytics scripts. If those practices change, this notice should be updated before deployment.

For vehicle safety decisions, verify recall status using the official NHTSA VIN checker.
