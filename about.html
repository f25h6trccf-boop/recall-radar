About Recall Radar

Recall Radar is an independent, free vehicle safety information project. Enter a VIN to decode basic vehicle information and review model-level recall campaigns available through public National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) APIs. You can save vehicles in your browser and compare campaign listings on return visits. A campaign listing is not proof that your specific VIN has an open recall. For VIN-specific status, use the official NHTSA recall lookup or contact the manufacturer. Recall Radar is not affiliated with or endorsed by NHTSA.
